A few common questions.

Is it okay if I’m new to exercise?

Absolutely. We’ll start with movements you can control and build from there. If you already have experience, I can adjust the workouts to challenge you.

What equipment do I need for Zoom?

None is required. We can use bodyweight, or adapt the session to equipment you already have at home.

Where do in-person sessions happen?

Sessions can be at your home or another agreed local spot in West Side San Jose, Los Gatos, Saratoga, Santa Clara, Campbell, or Cupertino.

How long are sessions, and what do they cost?

Sessions are 60 minutes: $60 in person or $45 online by Zoom.

Can we work around an injury or health concern?

Please tell me before we train. I don’t diagnose or treat injuries. Follow your healthcare provider’s guidance, and get medical clearance when needed.
