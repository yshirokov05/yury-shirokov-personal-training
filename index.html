01 / THE APPROACH

Good training should fit your life.

I keep training simple. Moving your own body with control should feel natural, and workouts should be easy to stick with. New to exercise? That’s fine—I’ll help you build from the basics. Have experience? Great—I’ll find ways to challenge you. Our bodies are made to move. Even on rough days, a little movement is better than none.
